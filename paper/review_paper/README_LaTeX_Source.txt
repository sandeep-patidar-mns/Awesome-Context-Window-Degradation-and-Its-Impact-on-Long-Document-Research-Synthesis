ACM CSUR-style Review Paper — Complete LaTeX Source

Contents:
- main.tex: complete LaTeX manuscript
- references.bib: BibTeX bibliography
- Figures/: conceptual framework, taxonomy, and figure source/resources
- Updated_Review_Paper.pdf: compiled manuscript

The manuscript uses the ACM acmart class in one-sided manuscript mode. The submitted copy intentionally omits the author name, headers, footers, and line numbering. Figures are placed on separate pages to avoid overlap and preserve readability. The bibliography uses ACM-Reference-Format.

Compile with pdflatex (twice) after the supplied main.bbl is available, or use Overleaf with the supplied .tex and .bib files.
